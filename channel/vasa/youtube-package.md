## Description

```
In 1628 Sweden launched the most heavily decorated warship in Europe.
It sailed 1,300 metres and capsized in a light breeze.

The part everyone forgets: the ship failed a stability test weeks
before it launched. Thirty men ran back and forth across the deck,
it heeled so violently the test was stopped — and the result was
never reported to the king.

Nobody was punished. The official verdict was that the ship was
"badly proportioned" and that God had allowed it. The stability
trial appears nowhere in the record.

333 years later she was raised from Stockholm harbour, over 98%
of her original timber intact, because the Baltic is too cold and
too fresh for the shipworm that eats wooden wrecks everywhere else.
She now draws about a million visitors a year.
```
