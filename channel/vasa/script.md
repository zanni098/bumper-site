# Video #1 — The warship that sank because nobody dared tell the king — Vasa, 1628

**Channel type:** history · **Style:** Editorial Motion Graphics · **Length:** 300s (30 blocks × 10s) · **Aspect:** 16:9

> Validated against the pipeline's own gate: `validate_motion_script.py --duration-seconds 300` → `"valid": true`.

## Through-line

**A single gilded lion-head gun port lid** (`lionlid`)

Introduced as a corroded relic in the cold open, then followed forward from raw lime log to carved face to gilded lid to the hatch that is deliberately opened for a ceremonial salute — the exact hole the sea comes through. It sinks with the ship, is sealed in silt, and is lifted again in 1961.

**Resolution:** The same lid, gold still legible, lit under museum glass in the payoff — the object that killed the ship is the object that made her famous.
